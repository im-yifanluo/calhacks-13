How to use
  • Use Hide UI at the top (or press H) to clear the view. A faint Show UI button stays available; tap it, double-tap the scene, or press H / Esc to restore controls. This also works in Wearer's view.
  • Drag to look around, scroll to zoom. Space plays or pauses, ← → step, [ ] switch takes.
  • Drag the scene to look around, pinch to zoom, and use the pad to walk.
  • The Camera button switches between free look and following the recorded wearer's view.
  • Pause, then drag an object and let go: it stays there, frozen with everything else, until you press Play. Each change makes a new take; the original is always kept.
  • On a Quest: Enter VR, point and pull the trigger to use the panel, and hold grip to grab. Drag the bar under the panel to move it; B or Y hides it.
